从提交审核到正式上线，花了好长时间。最初想把积累的想法尽可能完整地做出来，一次性新增了许多功能。开发时只想着多做一些，到了审核阶段，才发现这些改动也让审核变得复杂。版本包含的功能太多，需要解释功能、录制演示视频，再加上反复修改、提交，整个过程比预想中更漫长。

最后干脆把功能拆分开，一点点提交上去，从最早开始做的产品，到现在终于完整上线，PrimePlayer 比预想中走了更长的路。如今总算能以完整的样子和大家见面，接下来，继续把它做好。

作为最早开发的应用，PrimePlayer 寄托了很多期待，不只是设计、开发过程中的思考，也包括制作出一个完整好用的App，分享给全世界的喜悦。

从最早下载使用各种App的时候，就经常碰到各种满是广告和弹窗的App，尤其是关闭一个广告又弹出另一个的时候，更是让人恼火。所以当我有机会开发一款属于自己的App的时候，我也希望能带给你们更好的使用体验。没有层出不穷的广告，没有关不尽的弹窗，播放的功能全部开放，只留一些功能作为会员福利。免费用户不必被故意打扰，或者广告污染，会员用户得到更好的体验，所有人一起享受这款App带给大家的快乐。

当然我也能理解App开发者想要盈利的心情，维持App的运营和存在每年都需要一定的开支，任何App如果没有付费盈利一定是无法长久存在的。所以我也同样需要你的支持和帮助，通过分享出去和付费，让这个App可以长久存活下去，支持我继续维护、开发，设计更多有意思和有用的功能。

It took a long time to get from submission to release. I wanted to bring as many of my ideas to life as possible, so I added a lot of features at once. During development, I was focused on building more. It wasn’t until the review stage that I realized how much those additions had complicated the process. With so many features to explain, demo videos to record, and repeated revisions and resubmissions, everything took longer than expected.

In the end, I split the features into smaller releases and submitted them gradually. PrimePlayer was the first product I started working on, and getting it fully released took longer than I had imagined. Now that the complete app is finally available, I’ll keep working to make it better.

As the first app I developed, PrimePlayer carries a lot of my hopes. It represents the thought that went into its design and development, but also the joy of creating a complete, useful app and sharing it with the world.

Ever since I first started downloading and using apps, I’ve come across so many filled with ads and pop-ups. Few things are more frustrating than closing one ad only to have another appear. So when I had the chance to build an app of my own, I wanted to offer you a better experience. No endless ads. No constant pop-ups. All playback features are available to everyone, with only some additional features reserved for paid members. Free users shouldn’t face deliberate interruptions or intrusive ads, while paid members get an enhanced experience. Everyone should be able to enjoy the app.

Of course, I also understand why app developers need to earn money. Keeping an app running and available comes with yearly expenses, and without revenue, it’s difficult to sustain it over the long term. That’s why I need your support too. By sharing PrimePlayer with others or becoming a paid member, you can help keep it going and support my continued work on maintenance, development, and more interesting and useful features.
