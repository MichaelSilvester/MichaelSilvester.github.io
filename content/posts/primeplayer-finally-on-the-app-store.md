PrimePlayer 终于上架了。

从提交审核到正式上线，前后花了一个半月。最初想把积累的想法尽可能完整地做出来，于是一次性新增了不少功能。开发时只想着多做一些，到了审核阶段，才发现这些改动也让审核变得复杂。1.0.0 版本包含的功能太多，需要解释功能、录制演示视频，再加上反复修改、提交，整个过程比预想中更漫长。

哪吒怀胎三年才出生，PrimePlayer 花了一个半月才审核通过，以后它的代号就是“哪吒”了。

现在，PrimePlayer 总算正式上线了。从一个想法到真正上架，比预想中费了更多功夫。接下来，继续把它做好。

<!-- en -->
PrimePlayer is finally on the App Store.

It took a month and a half from submission to release. I wanted to bring as many of my ideas to life as possible, so I added quite a few features all at once. During development, I was focused on building more. It wasn’t until the review stage that I realized how much those additions had complicated the process. Version 1.0.0 had so many features that explaining them, recording demo videos, and making repeated revisions and resubmissions took much longer than expected.

Nezha took three years to be born in Chinese mythology. PrimePlayer took a month and a half to pass review. From now on, its codename is “Nezha.”

Now, PrimePlayer is officially available. Getting from an idea to an App Store release took more work than I expected. Next, I’ll keep working to make it better.
