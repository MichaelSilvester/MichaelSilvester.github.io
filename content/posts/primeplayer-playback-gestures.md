## 轻点、双击与长按

PrimePlayer 的播放页提供一组面向触屏的直接操作：

- 轻点画面显示或隐藏控制栏。
- 双击画面播放或暂停视频。
- 长按画面临时加速播放，松开后结束临时加速。

这些操作可以直接在视频画面上完成。

## 滑动调节

播放过程中还可以通过单指滑动调整进度、亮度和音量：

- 左右滑动可快退或快进。
- 在画面左侧上下滑动可调节屏幕亮度。
- 在画面右侧上下滑动可调节系统音量。

## 缩放与移动画面

PrimePlayer 支持双指捏合缩放视频画面，也可以用双指拖动画面位置。双指双击会把画面恢复到默认状态。

## 右侧快捷操作

播放页右侧提供三项快捷操作：

- 隔空播放：把内容投放到其他兼容设备。
- 画中画：离开播放页后继续观看。
- 截图：轻点保存当前画面，长按录制 GIF 动图。

以上内容均来自 PrimePlayer 当前的播放手势指南。

## Tap, double-tap, and touch and hold

PrimePlayer provides direct touch controls on its player screen:

- Tap the picture to show or hide the control bar.
- Double-tap to play or pause the video.
- Touch and hold for temporary faster playback, then release to end it.

These actions can be performed directly on the video.

## Swipe controls

Single-finger swipes adjust playback position, brightness, and volume:

- Swipe horizontally to seek backward or forward.
- Swipe vertically on the left side to adjust screen brightness.
- Swipe vertically on the right side to adjust system volume.

## Zoom and move the picture

PrimePlayer supports two-finger pinch-to-zoom and two-finger dragging to reposition the video. A two-finger double-tap restores the default view.

## Quick actions

Three quick actions are available on the right side of the player:

- AirPlay: send playback to another compatible device.
- Picture in Picture: keep watching after leaving the player screen.
- Capture: tap to save a screenshot, or touch and hold to record a GIF.

All details above come from PrimePlayer’s current playback gesture guide.
