## 一个媒体库，装下所有来源的视频

PrimePlayer 是一款适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的多格式视频播放器。无论视频来自系统相册、“文件”、其他手机或电脑，还是一条网络链接，都可以放进同一个媒体库中统一播放和整理。

应用内置 28 种界面语言，不含广告，不收集用户信息，也不进行跨 App 跟踪。

## 常见格式与字幕

PrimePlayer 支持 MP4、MOV、M4V、MKV、AVI、FLV、WebM、RM/RMVB、WMV、TS/M2TS 等常见格式，以及 AV1、H.264、H.265/HEVC、VP9 等常见编码。在设备支持时会使用硬件解码，兼顾流畅度与功耗。

对于光盘内容，可以导入 ISO/IMG 镜像，或 BDMV、VIDEO_TS、DVDMEDIA 目录，直接播放其中的主视频。

播放时可以切换音轨，加载 SRT、ASS/SSA、VTT、SUB/IDX 等外挂字幕，并在字幕与画面不同步时调整字幕延迟。

## 整理媒体库

媒体库使用文件夹和子文件夹整理视频，支持搜索、排序、重命名、批量操作和自定义封面。常看的内容可以加入收藏或播放列表，最近播放记录也能随时查看。

多选时可以直接选择文件夹，把其中所有视频（包括子文件夹）一并加入收藏、播放列表，或导出到系统相册和其他设备。AVI 视频导出到相册时会自动转换为兼容的 MP4。

## 内容保护

文件夹、播放列表和收藏可以使用密码、面容或指纹保护。在“设置—播放列表”中开启“自动加密列表”后，包含加密文件夹视频的播放列表和收藏也会自动受到保护。

受保护文件夹中的视频不会出现在播放记录中，也不会出现在主屏幕的“继续播放”入口里。

## 导入与传输

视频可以通过多种方式进入媒体库：

- 从系统相册批量导入，或从“文件”选择多个视频。
- 在相册、“文件”或其他支持系统共享的 App 中，通过共享菜单把视频发送到 PrimePlayer，并导入媒体库或指定文件夹。
- 手机与其他手机或电脑连接同一个 Wi-Fi 后，在对方浏览器中打开 PrimePlayer 提供的页面，上传视频。

传输也可以反向进行。在媒体库中多选视频后，可以通过浏览器传输到其他设备；“浏览器文件传输”工具则能在其他设备与 iPhone、iPad 的“文件”之间双向传递文件，并可使用临时账号保护访问。

各导入方式的具体说明，可以参考《[PrimePlayer 的视频导入与媒体整理功能](/journal/primeplayer-import-and-library/)》。

## 网络视频与离线观看

粘贴视频链接即可在线播放，也支持直播内容的在线播放。对于支持下载的视频，可以保存到本地，之后无网络时也能观看。设置中还可以选择自动下载策略，并管理蜂窝网络下的播放行为。

## 顺手的播放体验

播放页面向触屏设计，常用操作都能直接在画面上完成：

- 轻点显示或隐藏控制栏，双击播放或暂停。
- 左右滑动调整进度，在画面左侧、右侧上下滑动分别调节亮度和音量。
- 双指缩放、移动画面，双指双击恢复默认。
- 0.5x 至 4x 倍速播放；长按画面临时变速，长按倍速可以自定义，最高 8x。

此外，PrimePlayer 会自动记住每个视频的播放进度，并提供单次播放、单循环、顺序播放和循环播放四种模式；片头和片尾可以分别设置跳过时长。播放时还可以锁定控制层，避免误触；兼容的视频支持画中画和隔空播放（AirPlay），截图按钮轻点即可保存当前画面。

完整的手势说明见《[PrimePlayer 的播放手势与快捷操作](/journal/primeplayer-playback-gestures/)》。

## GIF 录制与编辑

播放时长按截图按钮即可录制 GIF，最长 15 秒，适合把视频里的有趣片段做成动态表情包。暂停状态和直播画面无法录制。

录制结束后，可以选择直接保存，也可以进入编辑器：

- 裁剪画面比例和时间范围。
- 调整分辨率、帧率和画质。
- 选择正常、倒放或倒放回弹三种循环方式，并设置播放速度。
- 按微信、WhatsApp 等目标平台限制文件大小，或沿用上一次的配置。
- 兼容的视频来源还可以导出为 MP4。

保存前可以预览真实的生成效果，结果会保存到系统相册。如果不想每次都进入编辑，可以在“设置—播放”中关闭“录制 GIF 后确认编辑”，录制后按预设的高清、标清或流畅画质直接保存。

## 媒体库迁移

换新手机或在两台设备之间同步时，可以使用“视频传输—工具—数据迁移”。通过面容或指纹验证后，PrimePlayer 会与附近的另一台 PrimePlayer 设备建立直连，传输全部或选中的视频、文件夹结构及文件夹保护设置。

数据在设备之间直接加密传输，不经过开发者服务器，也不使用 iCloud、不占用 iCloud 存储空间。双方可以同时互相发送，相同的视频不会被重复添加。传输完成前，需要保持 PrimePlayer 处于前台。

## 专属主题

除了保留系统原生外观的“经典”主题，PrimePlayer 还提供月白、暮山、春夏秋冬、潮汐、星河、极光、禅心、活字、熊猫、悟空、哪吒、雷霆、圣剑等多款主题。每款主题都可以在三档效果之间调节。

## One library for video from every source

PrimePlayer is a multi-format video player for iPhone and iPad. Whether a video comes from Photos, Files, another phone or computer, or a network link, it can be played and organized in the same media library.

The app is available in 28 interface languages. It has no ads, collects no user information, and does not track you across apps.

## Common formats and subtitles

PrimePlayer plays common formats including MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, AVI, FLV, WebM, RM/RMVB, WMV, and TS/M2TS, with support for codecs such as AV1, H.264, H.265/HEVC, and VP9. Hardware decoding is used when available for smooth, power-efficient playback.

For disc content, you can import ISO/IMG images or BDMV, VIDEO_TS, and DVDMEDIA folders and play the main video directly.

During playback, you can switch audio tracks, load external subtitles such as SRT, ASS/SSA, VTT, and SUB/IDX, and adjust subtitle timing when it is out of sync with the picture.

## Organize your library

The library organizes video with folders and subfolders, and supports search, sorting, renaming, batch actions, and custom covers. Add frequently watched videos to Favorites or playlists, and review recently played items at any time.

When selecting multiple items, you can include folders to add all of their videos, including those in subfolders, to Favorites or playlists, or export them to Photos and other devices. AVI videos are converted to compatible MP4 files when exported to Photos.

## Content protection

Folders, playlists, and Favorites can be protected with a password, Face ID, or Touch ID. Turn on “Automatically Encrypt Playlists” in Settings > Playlists, and playlists and Favorites containing videos from protected folders are protected automatically.

Videos in protected folders stay out of playback history and the Continue Playing shortcut on the Home Screen.

## Import and transfer

Video can enter the library in several ways:

- Batch import from Photos, or select multiple videos from Files.
- Share videos from Photos, Files, or other compatible apps to PrimePlayer through the share sheet, then import them into the library or a chosen folder.
- When the phone and another phone or computer are on the same Wi-Fi network, open the page provided by PrimePlayer in the other device's browser and upload video.

Transfers work in the other direction too. After selecting videos in the library, you can send them to other devices through a browser. The Browser File Transfer tool moves files both ways between another device and Files on your iPhone or iPad, with optional temporary account protection.

For details on each import method, see [Video import and media organization in PrimePlayer](/journal/primeplayer-import-and-library/).

## Online video and offline viewing

Paste a video link to stream it, including live streams. Videos that support downloading can be saved locally for viewing without a network connection. Settings also let you choose an automatic download strategy and manage playback over cellular data.

## Intuitive playback

The player is designed for touch, so common actions happen directly on the picture:

- Tap to show or hide the controls; double-tap to play or pause.
- Swipe horizontally to seek, and swipe vertically on the left or right side to adjust brightness or volume.
- Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to move the picture; double-tap with two fingers to reset.
- Play at 0.5x to 4x speed, or touch and hold for a temporary speed change at a customizable rate of up to 8x.

PrimePlayer also remembers where you left off in each video and offers four playback modes: Play Once, Repeat One, Play in Order, and Repeat All. Intro and outro skip durations can be set separately. You can lock the controls to prevent accidental touches, use Picture in Picture and AirPlay with compatible videos, and tap the capture button to save the current frame.

For the full gesture guide, see [Playback gestures and quick actions in PrimePlayer](/journal/primeplayer-playback-gestures/).

## GIF recording and editing

Touch and hold the capture button during playback to record a GIF of up to 15 seconds, an easy way to turn fun video moments into reaction GIFs. Recording is not available while playback is paused or for live streams.

After recording, you can save directly or open the editor to:

- Crop the frame and trim the time range.
- Adjust resolution, frame rate, and quality.
- Choose Normal, Reverse, or Boomerang looping and set the playback speed.
- Limit the file size for target platforms such as WeChat and WhatsApp, or reuse your previous settings.
- Export MP4 for compatible video sources.

You can preview the actual result before saving it to Photos. If you would rather skip the editor, turn off “Confirm editing after GIF recording” in Settings > Playback, and recordings will be saved directly at your preset High, Standard, or Smooth quality.

## Media library migration

When switching to a new phone or syncing two devices, use Video Transfer > Tools > Data Migration. After Face ID or Touch ID verification, PrimePlayer connects directly to a nearby PrimePlayer device and transfers all or selected videos, folder structure, and folder protection settings.

Data travels directly between devices over an encrypted connection, without passing through the developer's servers. Migration does not use iCloud or take up iCloud storage. Both devices can send at the same time, and identical videos are not added twice. Keep PrimePlayer in the foreground until the transfer finishes.

## Exclusive themes

Alongside the Classic theme, which keeps the native system look, PrimePlayer offers themes such as Moonlight, Dusk, the four seasons, Tide, Galaxy, Aurora, Zen, Letterpress, Panda, Wukong, Nezha, Thunderbolt, and Excalibur. Each theme has three effect levels.
