烬夜猎人适用于运行 iOS / iPadOS 17.0 或更高版本的 iPhone 和 iPad，采用横屏操作。英文名称为 Ashen Hunters。如需帮助，请联系 [MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com](mailto:MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com)，并注明“烬夜猎人”。

## 开始与通关

### 如何操作，能否离线游玩

左侧摇杆移动，武器自动攻击；右侧按钮释放角色主动技能。通过击败敌人、拾取经验和选择升级，组合武器、被动与进化，在约 20 分钟的远征中挑战最终首领。战斗可以离线进行；加载商品、购买、恢复购买和更新会员资格使用 Apple 服务，可能需要联网。

### 哪些内容免费，为什么地图打不开

两名角色、六件基础武器和完整单局免费。六张地图也全部免费，按荒墓庭园 → 血月荆林 → 沉没圣堂 → 熔烬铸堡 → 霜噬山隘 → 永夜王庭的顺序推进。

第一张地图默认开放。开始后续地图的新局前，需要在它之前的所有地图任意难度获胜；界面会提示最早尚未满足的地图。各地图的更高难度也需按通关进度开启。购买会员或内容包不会跳过这些要求。

### 武器怎样进化

在图鉴查看武器的进化配方，将武器升至 8 级、对应被动升至 3 级，并存活至少 10 分钟后，可在升级候选中选择符合条件的进化。进化需要满足配方和本局条件，单独购买武器包不会立即获得一局内的进化武器。

## 宠物、外观与补给

### 宠物有什么作用

宠物包包含首版三只宠物，分别辅助自动拾取、持续回血或攻击，每局最多携带一只。有效月度或年度会员以及终身会员也包含宠物访问资格。先在出战准备中选择宠物，再开始远征。

### 衣橱包为什么没有同时解锁角色

衣橱包永久解锁首版十二款皮肤，不增加角色数值；使用皮肤仍需拥有对应角色的访问资格。角色可通过猎人包或有效会员解锁。

### 补给怎么购买和携带，会员也要消耗魂烬吗

在出战准备的补给区域使用魂烬购买，每份消耗 1000 魂烬。购买增加库存，选中携带后在成功开始远征时按规则扣除。会员不会免除魂烬费用。魂烬来自游戏，当前没有用人民币购买魂烬或补给的消耗型内购。

## 暂停与保存

### 如何保存并继续远征

战斗中打开暂停页，可选择“继续远征”，或“保存并返回避难所”。存在未完成存档时，首页会显示“继续远征”。切到后台也会触发暂停与保存，返回后需手动继续。

请确认保存成功再退出。遇到保存错误时，保留 App 并联系支持，不要立即卸载重装。

### 换设备或卸载后能恢复进度吗

游戏进度、魂烬、补给和设置只保存在本机，当前没有开发者云存档、跨设备进度同步或存档导入导出。“恢复购买”只恢复符合条件的付费内容访问资格，不能恢复已删除的游戏进度。系统备份和设备迁移由 Apple 及你的设置管理，无法保证恢复结果。

## 购买与会员

### 永久包、订阅和终身会员有什么区别

猎人包、军械包、衣橱包和宠物包为一次性永久购买，分别解锁购买页列出的固定内容。月度和年度会员自动续订，有效期内访问全部已发布付费内容及期间新增内容。终身会员一次购买，持续访问本应用当前及后续发布的正式付费内容与功能。

会员到期不会取消单独购买的永久包，也不会删除已保存的养成与进度。地图和难度仍按正常通关要求开放。

### 已付款但内容没有开放怎么办

1. 确认设备可联网，并使用购买时的 Apple 账户。
2. 在游戏首页打开“商店”，选择“恢复购买”；价格无法加载时可选择“重新加载商品”。
3. 在 Apple 购买记录中确认交易已经完成；等待家长批准、取消或尚未完成的交易不代表已购买。
4. 仍未解决时，联系支持并提供商品名称、购买时间和必要的订单信息，遮挡无关的个人资料。

恢复购买不会再次收费，也不会恢复本地游戏存档。

### 如何取消订阅，终身购买会自动取消原订阅吗

在游戏商店选择“管理订阅”，或在系统“设置”> Apple 账户 >“订阅”中管理。取消续订通常保留访问资格至已付费周期结束。**购买终身会员不会自动取消月度或年度订阅；卸载 App 也不会取消订阅。** 请主动检查原订阅。参见 [Apple 订阅取消说明](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118428)。退款由 Apple 处理，可查看 [Apple 退款支持](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118223)。

## 设置与反馈

可在“设置”中切换语言、调整音乐与音效、触觉反馈、伤害数字、减少视觉特效、30 / 60 FPS、摇杆大小和透明度。游戏提供 32 种界面语言；网站支持文章提供中文和英文。运行不流畅时可先开启“减少视觉特效”或选择 30 FPS。

反馈时请提供 App 版本、设备型号、系统版本、所选语言、地图和难度、操作步骤及错误信息；必要时附上截图。购买问题请补充商品名称与购买时间。不要发送 Apple 账户密码、完整付款资料或无关的个人信息。

## Starting and progressing

### How do I play and does it work offline

Move with the left joystick. Weapons attack automatically; use the right button for your character's active skill. Defeat enemies, collect experience, and choose upgrades to combine weapons, passives, and evolutions in a run of about 20 minutes ending with a final boss. Combat works offline. Product loading, purchases, restoration, and membership updates use Apple services and may need internet access.

### What is free and why is a map locked

Two characters, six base weapons, and a full playable run are free. All six maps are also free, progressing through Graveyard Garden → Bloodmoon Thicket → Drowned Cathedral → Emberforge Citadel → Frostbite Pass → Court of Endless Night.

The first map opens immediately. Starting a new run on a later map requires victory on any difficulty in every preceding map. The game identifies the earliest missing prerequisite. Higher difficulties unlock through each map's progression. Memberships and packs do not skip these requirements.

### How do weapons evolve

Check evolution recipes in the Codex. Reach weapon level 8, obtain its matching passive at level 3, and survive at least 10 minutes. An eligible evolution can then appear among upgrade choices. Buying a weapon pack grants content access; it does not immediately equip an evolved weapon in a run.

## Pets, skins, and supplies

### What do pets do

The Pets Pack includes three launch pets for automatic pickup, healing, or attack support. Bring at most one per run. Active monthly or annual membership and lifetime membership also include pet access. Select a pet in run preparation before starting.

### Why does the Wardrobe Pack not unlock a character

The Wardrobe Pack permanently unlocks twelve launch skins without stat bonuses. You still need access to each skin's character through the Hunters Pack or active membership.

### How do I buy and equip supplies, and do members spend Soul Embers

Buy supplies in run preparation for 1,000 Soul Embers each. Buying increases inventory. Selected supplies are consumed under the displayed rules when a run successfully starts. Membership does not remove the Soul Ember cost. Soul Embers come from gameplay; the current game does not sell Soul Embers or supplies through consumable in-app purchases.

## Pausing and saving

### How do I save and continue a run

Open the pause screen during combat to continue or choose SAVE & RETURN. The home screen shows CONTINUE when an unfinished save exists. Moving the app to the background also pauses and saves the run. Resume manually after returning.

Confirm that saving succeeds before leaving. If you see a save error, keep the app installed and contact support.

### Can I recover progress after deleting the app or changing devices

Progress, Soul Embers, supplies, and settings stay on your device. The game currently has no developer cloud saves, cross-device progress sync, or save import and export. Restore Purchases restores eligible paid-content access, not deleted progress. Apple manages system backups and migration according to your settings; recovery is not guaranteed.

## Purchases and membership

### How do packs, subscriptions, and lifetime membership differ

The Hunters, Armory, Wardrobe, and Pets packs are one-time permanent purchases covering the fixed content on their purchase pages. Monthly and annual memberships renew automatically and provide all released paid content, including additions released during the active period. Lifetime membership is a one-time purchase providing ongoing access to this app's current and future released paid content and features.

Membership expiration does not remove independently purchased packs or delete saved upgrades and progress. Maps and difficulties still require normal victories.

### What if I paid but content is unavailable

1. Connect to the internet and use the Apple Account used for purchase.
2. Open Store from the home screen, then select Restore purchases. If prices do not load, try Reload products.
3. Check Apple's purchase history to confirm completion. Pending approval, canceled, or unfinished transactions do not grant a completed purchase.
4. If the issue remains, contact support with the product name, purchase time, and necessary order information. Hide unrelated personal information.

Restoring does not charge you again or recover local game saves.

### How do I cancel, and does lifetime membership cancel a subscription

Choose Manage subscriptions in the game store or go to Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Turning off renewal generally retains access until the paid period ends. **Lifetime membership does not automatically cancel an existing monthly or annual subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel one either.** Review your previous subscriptions. See [Apple's cancellation instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). Apple handles refunds; see [Apple refund support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223).

## Settings and feedback

Settings lets you change language, music and sound levels, haptics, damage numbers, reduced effects, 30 / 60 FPS, joystick size, and opacity. The game provides 32 interface languages; these support articles are available in Chinese and English. If performance is slow, try Reduce effects or 30 FPS.

When reporting a problem, include the app version, device model, system version, selected language, map and difficulty, steps, and error messages. Attach a screenshot when useful. For purchase issues, include the product name and purchase time. Do not send Apple Account passwords, complete payment information, or unrelated personal details.
