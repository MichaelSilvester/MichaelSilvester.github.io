## 添加壁纸素材

MagicDesk 支持两类添加方式：

- 拖入文件或点击“添加文件”，导入本地图片、GIF 和视频。
- 点击“添加 URL”，添加在线图片、视频或网页。

导入后的内容会进入 MagicDesk 的壁纸库。

## 整理壁纸库

壁纸库提供搜索、标签、收藏和自定义列表。素材可以拖动调整顺序，也可以通过多选批量收藏、加入列表或删除。

## 设置到一个或多个显示器

选中素材后，可以先在右侧预览并选择填充、适应或拉伸等适配方式，再把它设置到全部屏幕或指定屏幕。连接多台显示器时，每块屏幕可以分别使用不同的壁纸。

## 控制动态壁纸

主窗口和菜单栏都可以暂停、继续、切换上一张或下一张壁纸，以及控制声音。动态壁纸也可以暂时停止并在需要时重新开启。

全局快捷键支持暂停或继续、开启或关闭动态壁纸、切换上一张或下一张以及开关声音；这些快捷键可以在设置中修改或停用。

## 启动与智能省电

MagicDesk 可以设置为登录后自动启动。开启智能省电后，应用会在游戏运行、全屏工作或 Mac 使用电池时自动暂停动态内容，并在条件解除后恢复播放。

## Add wallpaper media

MagicDesk supports two ways to add content:

- Drag files in or choose “Add Files” to import local images, GIFs, and videos.
- Choose “Add URL” to add an online image, video, or webpage.

Imported items appear in the MagicDesk wallpaper library.

## Organize the wallpaper library

The library provides search, tags, favorites, and custom lists. Items can be reordered by dragging, and multi-selection can favorite, add to lists, or delete several items at once.

## Set one or more displays

After selecting an item, preview it and choose a fill, fit, or stretch mode, then assign it to every display or a specific display. When multiple displays are connected, each display can use a different wallpaper.

## Control live wallpapers

The main window and menu bar can pause or resume playback, move to the previous or next wallpaper, and control sound. Live wallpapers can also be stopped temporarily and enabled again later.

Global shortcuts cover pause or resume, enable or disable live wallpapers, previous or next wallpaper, and sound. These shortcuts can be changed or disabled in Settings.

## Launch and smart power saving

MagicDesk can launch automatically after login. With smart power saving enabled, it pauses moving content while a game is running, an app is in full screen, or the Mac is using battery power, then resumes when those conditions end.
