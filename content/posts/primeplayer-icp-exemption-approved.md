10 月 30 日，PrimePlayer 的 ICP 豁免申请正式获得苹果批准。

在[上一篇文章](/journal/primeplayer-icp-exemption/)里，我还在等待申请结果。因为网络视频和 IPTV 功能，PrimePlayer 需要向苹果说明网络请求的用途、内容来源和数据处理方式。现在，这件事终于有了结果。

## 这次申请说明了什么

本次申请的基础，是 PrimePlayer 不使用开发者自建的后台服务，也不把用户信息上传到开发者服务器。如果应用自建后台、向那里上传和保存用户信息，就无法再以“无开发者后台、无用户数据上传”这一前提申请同样的豁免。

PrimePlayer 的网络视频和 IPTV 功能，都是由设备直接访问你添加的内容来源。应用负责解析、整理和播放，视频与频道请求不需要经过我的服务器中转。我也没有运营一个接收大家私人媒体或观看记录的后台。

## 隐私保护，落实在日常使用里

这也是我一直希望坚持的设计方式：

- 无需注册账户或登录，不必为了播放自己的视频先提交个人资料。
- 本地视频、播放历史、文件夹和播放列表保存在设备上，不上传到开发者服务器。
- 不包含广告、用户跟踪或行为分析，不通过观看习惯建立用户画像。
- 浏览器传输与附近设备间迁移直接在相关设备之间进行，文件不经过开发者服务器。

当你播放网络视频或 IPTV 频道时，内容来源服务器仍会接收 IP 地址等正常连接所需的信息；App Store 购买和恢复购买由 Apple 处理。各项功能的数据处理方式，也会继续在[隐私政策](/journal/primeplayer-privacy-policy/)中说明清楚。

## 欢迎放心下载和体验

从提交申请到获得批准，又走完了一段上架流程。也借这个机会，把 PrimePlayer 如何对待个人隐私讲得更明白一些：你的私人媒体和观看记录，不需要交到我手里。

欢迎大家放心[下载和体验 PrimePlayer](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6799107071)。希望它能成为一个用着顺手、尊重个人隐私的视频工具。我会继续维护和改进它，让播放、整理和传输视频都更方便，也认真守住这些数据处理原则。

On October 30, Apple approved PrimePlayer’s ICP exemption application.

In the [previous article](/journal/primeplayer-icp-exemption/?lang=en), I was still waiting for a response. PrimePlayer’s online video and IPTV features meant explaining the purpose of its network requests, where content comes from, and how data is handled. That process now has a result.

## What the application explained

The application was based on PrimePlayer having no developer-operated backend and uploading no user information to a developer server. An app that operates a backend and uploads or stores user information there could no longer apply for the same exemption on that basis.

For online video and IPTV, your device connects directly to the content sources you add. PrimePlayer parses, organizes, and plays that content without routing video or channel requests through my servers. I do not operate a backend that receives your private media or viewing history.

## Privacy in everyday use

These are the design choices I want to keep:

- No account or sign-in is required, so playing your own videos does not require submitting personal details first.
- Local videos, viewing history, folders, and playlists stay on your device and are not uploaded to developer-operated servers.
- There are no ads, user tracking, or behavioral analytics, and viewing habits are not used to build user profiles.
- Browser transfers and nearby-device migration happen directly between the participating devices, without sending files through developer-operated servers.

When you play online videos or IPTV channels, the source server still receives information needed for the connection, such as your IP address. App Store purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple. The [Privacy Policy](/journal/primeplayer-privacy-policy/?lang=en) explains how data is handled across these features.

## Download and try it with confidence

Getting from application to approval completes another part of the release process. It is also a chance to explain how PrimePlayer treats personal privacy: your private media and viewing history do not need to be handed over to me.

You are welcome to [download and try PrimePlayer](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6799107071) with confidence. I hope it becomes a useful video player that respects your privacy. I will keep improving playback, organization, and transfers while maintaining these principles for handling your data.
