Texturo 新增了语音转文字和版本历史：想法可以直接说出来，写过的内容也能随时回头查看。无论是先把灵感记下，还是在反复修改后找回一句喜欢的话，写作都能更从容一些。

## 语音转文字

突然想到一段开头，或者手边不方便打字时，可以在文稿编辑器右上角的“更多”菜单中打开“语音转文字”。对着设备说话，识别出的文字会直接出现在当前光标位置；说完后，还可以接着修改措辞和排版。

语音面板可以在编辑区域内拖动，不会一直挡住正在写的内容。听写时可以随时暂停、继续，也能自行选择“识别语言”。让识别语言与正在说的话一致，有助于更准确地转成文字；需要用另一种语言记录时，也可以直接切换。

## 版本历史

写作时难免会有“刚删掉的那段其实更好”的时候。现在可以从编辑器的“更多”菜单，或文稿库的文稿操作菜单进入“版本历史”，按时间找到相关更改，比较段落的“更改前”“更改后”和“当前”内容。

如果只想找回其中一段，选中需要的内容，再切到“完整内容”预览整篇文稿的结果，确认后应用即可。未选中的部分仍保留当前内容，不必为了恢复一句话把整篇文章退回旧版本。想留一份文字备用，也可以使用“复制全文”。

自动保存会把密集修改归为一组，不会每打一个字就生成一条记录。在自动保存模式下，如果希望接下来的修改单独成组，可以使用“保存版本”。重要的历史记录可以“锁定”，避免被自动清理；如果手动删除记录，当前文稿不会受影响，但以后就无法再通过那条记录找回内容了。

Texturo now includes Speech to Text and Version History: you can speak an idea straight into a document and revisit what you have written. Whether you are capturing a thought or looking for a line you liked better before an edit, writing can feel a little more relaxed.

## Speech to Text

When an opening line comes to mind or typing is inconvenient, open “Speech to Text” from the editor’s More menu. Speak to your device and the recognized words appear at the current cursor position. You can refine the wording and formatting afterward.

Move the speech panel around the editor so it stays out of the way. You can pause and resume dictation, and choose the “Recognition Language” yourself. Matching it to the language you are speaking can help improve transcription accuracy; you can switch languages when you need to write in another one.

## Version History

Sometimes you delete a paragraph, then realize you preferred the original. Open “Version History” from the editor’s More menu or a document’s action menu in the library. Find the change by time and compare its “Before,” “After,” and “Now” text.

If you only want one passage back, select it and switch to “Full Text” to preview the whole document before applying the change. Everything you leave unselected stays as it is, so recovering a sentence does not mean rolling back the entire document. You can also use “Copy All” to keep a separate text copy.

Automatic saving groups closely spaced edits rather than creating an entry for every keystroke. While automatic saving is on, use “Save Version” if you want your next edits in a separate group. You can “Lock” an important history entry to protect it from automatic cleanup. Manually deleting an entry leaves the current document untouched, but you will no longer be able to restore content from that entry.
