## 从一篇文稿开始

Diple 是一款适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的 Markdown 与纯文本写作工具。你可以随手记录灵感、写日记和笔记，也可以慢慢完成更长的草稿。它不要求注册账户或登录，不使用开发者运营的云服务器，也不提供 iCloud 或其他云同步，内容保存在设备本地。

## 四种显示模式，适配不同的写作习惯

编辑器提供沉浸式（所见即所得）、实时预览、纯预览和源码四种显示模式，可以在文稿编辑界面中随时切换。无论是专注写作、边写边看排版效果，还是核对原始 Markdown 语法，都能找到合适的方式。

格式工具栏支持标题、加粗、斜体、行内代码、代码块（含多种编程语言的语法高亮）、引用、无序列表、有序列表、任务列表、链接、图片、删除线、分隔线和表格，也可以直接输入标准 Markdown 语法。

想到内容却不方便打字时，可以使用语音转文字，并自行选择识别语言。写作过程中，版本历史可以帮助你比较更改前后的内容，并只找回真正需要的段落。

## 用文件夹和合集整理内容

文稿可以按文件夹分类管理，也可以自定义排序，或按名称、修改时间、创建时间排序。开启“修改置顶”后，编辑并保存的文稿会自动移到当前文件夹顶部，方便找到最近处理的内容。

当几篇文稿开始形成一个主题时，可以把它们编成合集，设置封面、简介、章节、编号和阅读顺序。选择“加入”会将文稿归档到合集文件夹；选择“引用”则保留文稿原来的位置，并让合集始终显示同一篇文稿的最新内容。目录跳转、连续阅读和阅读位置记录，让分散的章节更接近一本完整的作品。

## 让写作空间更像自己

Diple 提供多种主题和稿纸，并适配浅色与深色外观。你可以为编辑与阅读选择不同风格，也可以用自己的图片制作稿纸，再搭配喜欢的 App 图标。

## 加密文件夹，保护私密内容

升级到 Diple Pro 后，可以为文件夹设置密码，或在设备支持时启用 Face ID / Touch ID。App 进入后台时，加密文件夹会自动重新锁定，密码只存储在设备本地。

## 删除与恢复

删除的文件夹会先进入废纸篓，等待自动清理或手动恢复；文稿的处理方式（移入废纸篓或直接永久删除）可以在设置中选择。开启“废纸篓加密”后，浏览包含加密文件夹内容的废纸篓还需要通过生物识别验证。

## 导出与分享

文稿可以导出为 Markdown、TXT、Word 或 PDF，也可以通过系统分享发送到其他 App 或设备。需要整理多篇内容时，还可以批量导出、导出整个文件夹，或把一整册合集合并成一个文件。

Diple 不含广告、第三方行为分析 SDK 或跟踪代码，也不会把文稿内容上传到开发者运营的服务器。让它成为你的一个安静、专注、值得信赖的写作工具。

## Start with a document

Diple is a Markdown and plain-text writing tool for iPhone and iPad. Capture a quick idea, keep a journal or notes, or gradually complete a longer draft. It requires no account or sign-in, uses no developer-operated cloud servers, and offers no iCloud or other cloud sync, so your content stays on your device.

## Four display modes for different writing habits

The editor offers four display modes — immersive (WYSIWYG), live preview, preview-only, and source — that you can switch between at any time while editing. Whether you want to focus on writing, watch formatted output as you type, or check the raw Markdown syntax, there's a mode for it.

The formatting toolbar covers headings, bold, italic, inline code, code blocks with syntax highlighting for multiple programming languages, quotes, unordered lists, ordered lists, task lists, links, images, strikethrough, horizontal rules, and tables — or you can type standard Markdown syntax directly.

When an idea arrives and typing is inconvenient, use Speech to Text and choose the recognition language yourself. As a document changes, Version History helps you compare edits and restore only the passages you need.

## Organize with folders and Collections

Documents can be grouped into folders, arranged in a custom order, or sorted by name, modification time, or creation time. With "Move Edited Documents to Top" enabled, a document you edit and save automatically moves to the top of its folder, making recently touched content easy to find again.

When several documents grow into one subject, bring them into a Collection with a cover, summary, chapters, numbering, and a reading order. “Add” files a document inside the Collection folder. “Reference” leaves it in its original location while the Collection continues to show the latest version of that same document. A table of contents, continuous reading, and saved reading position make separate chapters feel like one complete work.

## Make the writing space your own

Diple includes themes and Writing Paper designed for Light and Dark appearance. Choose different looks for editing and reading, create Writing Paper from your own image, and pair it with an app icon you like.

## Encrypted folders for private content

After upgrading to Diple Pro, you can protect a folder with a password, or enable Face ID / Touch ID where the device supports it. Encrypted folders automatically re-lock when the App enters the background, and passwords are stored only on your device.

## Deletion and recovery

Deleted folders move to Trash first, where they wait for automatic cleanup or manual recovery; how documents are deleted — moved to Trash or removed immediately — is a setting you control. With "Protect Trash" enabled, browsing Trash that contains items from encrypted folders requires biometric authentication.

## Export and share

Export documents as Markdown, TXT, Word, or PDF, or send them to another app or device through the system share sheet. When working with several pieces, you can also export in batches, export a whole folder, or combine an entire Collection into one file.

Diple has no ads, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no tracking code, and it never uploads your documents to a developer-operated server. I hope it can be a quiet, focused, and trustworthy writing tool.
