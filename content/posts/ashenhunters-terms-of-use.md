Free content includes two characters, six base weapons, and a full playable run. All six maps are free, progressing through Graveyard Garden, Bloodmoon Thicket, Drowned Cathedral, Emberforge Citadel, Frostbite Pass, and Court of Endless Night.

The first map opens immediately. Starting a new run on a later map requires victory on any difficulty in every preceding map. Higher difficulties unlock through each map's progression. Memberships and permanent packs do not remove these requirements.

Earn Soul Embers through gameplay and spend them on features such as talents and run supplies. Supply purchases and loadouts consume resources under the displayed rules; each supply currently costs 1,000 Soul Embers. ## 3. Permanent packs

Current permanent packs are one-time, non-consumable purchases without automatic renewal:

- **Hunters Pack:** Four additional characters.
- **Armory Pack:** Six additional weapons and their evolutions.
- **Wardrobe Pack:** Twelve launch skins without stat bonuses. Their characters must be unlocked separately.
- **Pets Pack:** Three launch pets providing automatic pickup, healing, or attack support. Bring at most one per run.

Each pack covers only the fixed content identified on its purchase page and does not automatically include other packs added in the future. Packs and membership access are calculated separately. Membership expiration does not remove independently purchased packs.

Membership grants content access without skipping map, difficulty, or normal progression requirements. Using content during membership does not turn it into a separately purchased permanent pack. Membership applies only to this game.

Prices, currency, periods, and offer eligibility follow the app's purchase pages and Apple's purchase confirmation. Subscriptions renew under the confirmed terms unless canceled. **Buying lifetime membership does not automatically cancel an existing monthly or annual subscription.** Review and manage the previous subscription to avoid further renewal charges. Deleting the app, deleting saves, or stopping play does not cancel subscriptions.
