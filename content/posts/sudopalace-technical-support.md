数独闯关 SudoPalace 适用于运行 iOS / iPadOS 17.0 及以上系统的 iPhone 和 iPad，支持英文和简体中文。如需帮助，请联系 [MichaelSilvesterCN+SudoPalace@gmail.com](mailto:MichaelSilvesterCN+SudoPalace@gmail.com)。

## 关卡与星级

### 哪些关卡免费，为什么下一关打不开

五大难度各有 100 关，每个难度前 10 关免费。同一难度内需先通过上一关，或用“填充答案”跳过，才能开启下一关。

从第 11 关起，还需使用钻石单独解锁或拥有有效的 SudoPalace Pro 会员。Pro 免除钻石解锁费用，但仍需按顺序挑战。钻石解锁费用按难度为：简单／中等 1 钻，困难／专家 2 钻，大师 3 钻。

### 怎样获得三星

通关时错误次数为 0，未使用“提示”或“填格”，并在该难度的目标时间内完成，即可获得三星。目标时间为：简单 5 分钟、中等 8 分钟、困难 13 分钟、专家 20 分钟、大师 30 分钟。

笔记和撤销本身不影响三星资格，但撤销不会减少已记录的错误次数。每局达到 3 次错误会失败；每局可花费 1 钻石复活一次，错误次数及计时清零，清除填错的数字，保留其余进度。复活后按新的计时和错误次数评星，未用提示或填格且符合时限时仍可获得三星。

### 为什么重玩没有再次获得奖励

关卡金币在首次通关时发放；重玩提高星级时只补发差额，没有提高就不再发放。每关首次获得三星会发钻石，重复三星不会再次发放。首次挑战即三星另有额外奖励，首次挑战用掉后不能通过重新开始找回。

每日挑战只在当天首次通关时发放奖励。已通关后重玩不再发奖励。使用“填充答案”跳关不获得星级、金币或钻石；跳过的关卡可之后重玩。

## 提示、笔记与教程

### 提示和填格有什么区别

“提示”每次消耗 25 金币，会直接填入系统找到的一步并显示说明；棋盘中有错误时会先更正错误。遇到无法由内置基础提示技巧说明的局面时，提示会直接给出某格答案。

“填格”每次消耗 1 钻石，把当前选中的可填写格直接填成正确答案。Pro 会员同样支付这些道具费用。

### 为什么不能擦除数字，笔记又为什么消失了

题目给出的数字及已填对的数字不能直接修改或擦除，撤销可以回退相应的历史操作。开启“笔记”后，数字键改为添加或移除候选数；每次进入游戏或重新开始时，笔记模式默认关闭。

“自动清除笔记”开启时，填入正确数字会移除同行、同列、同宫中相同数字的笔记。可以在“设置”中关闭。

### 从哪里学习数独技巧

从首页进入“技巧教程”。8 节课程全部免费，每节包含介绍、分步演示和互动练习，覆盖规则、显性与隐性唯一数、区块排除、数对、X-Wing 和 XY-Wing。学习完成后不发放金币或钻石。

## 每日挑战与保存进度

### 每日挑战为什么不是我想要的难度

当天难度从简单到你曾正常通关的最高难度之间选择；跳过关卡不提高这个范围。尚未正常通关时出简单题。当天第一次打开挑战后难度固定，之后通关更高难度也不会改变当天的题目。

### 如何继续游戏，切到后台后为什么暂停

游戏在操作后及定期自动保存。可从首页“继续游戏”或对应关卡继续未完成的棋盘。切到后台会自动暂停，暂停期间不计时，棋盘会被遮挡。

**进度与货币只保存在本机，不在设备之间同步。请先联系支持，不要为了排查问题而删除 App。** 当前没有云存档或存档导入导出功能；删除 App 后不能通过“恢复购买”找回这些数据。

## 会员与购买

### Pro 包含什么，每日礼包为什么领不了

Pro 提供周度、月度、年度订阅和终身一次性购买，可免除全部 500 关的钻石解锁费用，并领取会员每日礼包。仍需按顺序闯关，提示、填格、复活和跳关仍按规则消耗货币。

首页的“会员每日礼包”包含金币与钻石，具体数量以 App 内显示为准。每天可手动领取一次，未领取不累计。请连接网络，确认会员仍有效，让 App 完成 Apple 权益验证后再尝试；离线缓存的会员状态不能用于领取礼包。

### 已付款但会员未生效，或者金币和钻石没有到账

- 确认设备可联网，且 App Store 使用原购买的 Apple 账户。
- 检查 Apple 购买记录，区分已完成付款、等待批准与取消的交易；等待批准不代表已完成购买。
- 会员未生效时，在会员页或商店点击“恢复购买”。这可恢复有效订阅或终身权益。
- 金币或钻石未到账时，保留 App，联网重新打开，让未完成的交易继续处理；仍未到账请联系支持，并附商品名称、购买时间和必要的订单信息。

金币与钻石是消耗型购买，已完成交易发放的余额无法通过恢复购买重新发放。恢复会员也不会恢复存档或钻石解锁记录。参见 [Apple 恢复购买说明](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/108096)。

### 如何取消订阅或申请退款

可从“设置”中的会员卡进入会员页，使用“管理订阅”；也可在系统“设置”> Apple 账户 >“订阅”中取消。卸载 App 不会取消订阅，终身一次性购买不自动续费。参见 [Apple 订阅取消说明](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118428)。

退款由 Apple 处理，请参考 [Apple 退款支持](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118223)。如只是购买未到账，请先保留 App 并联系支持排查。

## 语言、外观与问题反馈

在“设置”>“系统”中调整“语言”和“深色模式”。语言可选跟随系统、简体中文或 English，切换后游戏界面立即生效；部分系统界面可能需下次启动后更新。

反馈问题时，请注明 数独闯关 SudoPalace，并提供 App 版本、设备型号、系统版本、操作步骤及错误信息。游戏问题可补充难度、关卡号、是否每日挑战及截图。购买问题请说明会员或货币商品名称与购买时间，遮挡截图中无关的个人信息，不要发送账户密码或完整付款资料。

## Levels and stars

### Which levels are free and why is the next level locked

Each of the five difficulties has 100 levels, with its first 10 free. Within a difficulty, clear the previous level or fill its answers to skip it before the next level opens.

From level 11 onward, also unlock the level with diamonds or hold active SudoPalace Pro membership. Pro removes the diamond cost but retains sequential progression. Individual unlocks cost 1 diamond for Easy or Medium, 2 for Hard or Expert, and 3 for Master.

### How do I earn three stars

Finish with zero recorded mistakes, no Hint or Fill Cell use, and within the difficulty’s target time: 5 minutes for Easy, 8 for Medium, 13 for Hard, 20 for Expert, and 30 for Master.

Notes and Undo do not themselves disqualify three stars, but Undo does not reduce recorded mistakes. Three mistakes cause failure. Once per game, spend 1 diamond to revive: this resets mistakes and the timer, clears incorrect entries, and retains other progress. Stars then use the new timer and mistake count. Three stars remain possible if you use no Hint or Fill Cell and finish within the target time.

### Why did replaying not award more rewards

Level coins are awarded on the first clear. Improving your star rating later awards only the difference; an unchanged rating awards nothing more. Each level awards diamonds only for its first three-star clear. An extra bonus applies to three stars on the first attempt; restarting cannot recover a first attempt already used.

Daily Challenge rewards are awarded only on the first clear that day. Replays give no further rewards. Filling answers to skip a level gives no stars, coins, or diamonds. You may replay a skipped level later.

## Hint, Notes, and tutorials

### What is the difference between Hint and Fill Cell

Hint costs 25 coins, directly fills a step found by the app, and displays an explanation. It corrects an existing wrong entry first. If the built-in basic hint techniques cannot explain the position, it provides a cell’s answer directly.

Fill Cell costs 1 diamond and fills the correct answer into the currently selected editable cell. Pro members pay the same tool costs.

### Why can I not erase a number and why did Notes disappear

Given numbers and correctly entered numbers cannot be edited or erased directly. Undo can reverse the relevant historical move. In Notes mode, number buttons add or remove candidates. Notes mode starts off each time you enter or restart a game.

With Auto-Remove Notes enabled, a correct entry removes that number from Notes in the same row, column, and box. Turn this off in Settings if preferred.

### Where can I learn Sudoku techniques

Open Technique Tutorials from the home screen. All eight lessons are free and include an introduction, step-by-step demonstration, and interactive practice. They cover rules, Naked and Hidden Singles, Locked Candidates, pairs, X-Wing, and XY-Wing. Completing lessons does not award coins or diamonds.

## Daily Challenge and saved games

### Why does Daily Challenge have a different difficulty than I expected

The difficulty is chosen between Easy and the highest difficulty you have cleared normally. Skipped levels do not increase this range. If you have no normal clears, the challenge is Easy. The day’s difficulty is fixed when you first open it; later clears at higher difficulties do not change that day’s puzzle.

### How do I resume a game and why does backgrounding pause it

The app saves after moves and at regular intervals. Resume an unfinished board through Continue Game on the home screen or its level entry. Backgrounding pauses the game automatically. The timer stops while paused and the board is obscured.

**Progress and currency stay on this device and do not synchronize across devices. Contact support before deleting the app to troubleshoot.** There is currently no cloud save or saved-game import or export. Restore Purchases cannot recover this data after the app is deleted.

## Membership and purchases

### What does Pro include and why can I not claim my daily gift

Pro offers weekly, monthly, and annual subscriptions or a lifetime one-time purchase. It removes diamond unlock costs for all 500 levels and provides a daily member gift. Sequential progression still applies, and Hint, Fill Cell, revival, and skipping retain their currency costs.

Member Daily Gift on the home screen includes coins and diamonds; quantities are shown in the app. Claim it manually once per day. Unclaimed gifts do not accumulate. Connect to the internet, confirm your membership is active, and allow the app to verify Apple entitlements before retrying. Offline cached membership cannot claim a gift.

### I paid but membership is inactive or currency is missing

- Check internet access and that the App Store uses the original purchasing Apple Account.
- Check Apple purchase history for completed, pending approval, or canceled transactions. Pending approval is not a completed purchase.
- For missing membership, choose Restore Purchases on the membership page or in Shop to restore active subscriptions or lifetime access.
- For missing coins or diamonds, keep the app installed and reopen it online so unfinished transactions can be processed. If they remain missing, contact support with the product name, purchase time, and necessary order information.

Coins and diamonds are consumable purchases. Balances delivered by completed transactions cannot be reissued through Restore Purchases. Restoring membership also does not recover saved games or diamond unlock records. See [Apple’s purchase restoration guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108096).

### How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund

Open the membership page from the membership card in Settings and choose Manage Subscription, or use system Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime access does not renew automatically. See [Apple’s cancellation instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).

Apple handles refunds. See [Apple’s refund support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). For a missing purchase, keep the app installed and contact support to troubleshoot first.

## Language, appearance, and feedback

Change Language and Dark Mode under Settings > System. Language supports System, 简体中文, and English. The game interface updates immediately; some system interfaces may update after the next launch.

When reporting an issue, name SudoPalace and include the app version, device model, operating system version, steps, and error message. For game issues, add the difficulty, level number, whether it was Daily Challenge, and a screenshot. For purchase issues, provide the membership or currency product and purchase time. Redact unrelated personal information and never send account passwords or complete payment details.
