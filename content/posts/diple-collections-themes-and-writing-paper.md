在[上一篇文章](/journal/diple-speech-to-text-and-version-history/)的最后，我们写下：“接下来还准备为 Diple 做更炫酷的页面效果和交互……敬请期待。”现在，这次更新终于来了。

这一次，Diple 不只是增加几个设置选项，而是从文稿如何组织、如何阅读，到整个 App 看起来是什么样子，都有了新的变化。

## 把零散文稿编成合集

一篇文稿可以是一则灵感、一天的日记或一章草稿。当它们逐渐连成一个主题，就可以创建“合集”，为内容设置名称、简介和封面，再用章节、编号与自定义顺序把文稿组织起来。

把已有文稿放进合集时，可以选择“加入”或“引用”。“加入”会将文稿归档到合集文件夹；“引用”则让文稿留在原来的位置，合集里显示的仍是同一篇文稿，之后无论从哪一边编辑，内容都会保持同步。

合集不只是一个分类入口。你可以通过章节目录快速跳转，在多篇文稿之间连续阅读，并从上次停下的位置继续。需要把内容带到 Diple 之外时，也可以按照当前章节和顺序，将整册合集整理成一份文档导出。

## 让整个 Diple 换一种气质

新的主题会改变文稿库、合集、设置和其他页面的整体氛围。水墨保留宣纸留白与墨色层次，经典延续熟悉的系统外观；月白、竹影、暮山、熊猫、禅心和悟空则带来各自不同的颜色、图形与字体感受。

每套主题都同时照顾浅色与深色外观。你可以选一套安静耐看的风格长期使用，也可以随着季节、心情或正在写的内容随时更换。

## 给编辑和阅读页面铺一张稿纸

主题决定整个 App 的气质，稿纸则专门改变文稿页面。宣纸、石墨、羊皮纸、青瓷、靛蓝、樱粉等不同配色，会应用到正文、标题、引用、代码块、表格、链接和光标，让编辑与阅读拥有更完整的视觉层次。

如果预设稿纸还不够贴近想要的感觉，也可以从自己的图片制作稿纸。稿纸只改变页面呈现，不会写入或改动文稿正文，之后仍可以随时切换。

## 连主屏幕上的图标也可以自己选

App 图标也准备了不同的颜色和形象。从经典、朱砂、青瓷、墨夜，到翠竹、毛笔、铅笔、书签等图标，可以选择一个更适合自己主屏幕的 Diple。

这次更新还加入了从相册或文件插入图片，并调整了引用、代码块、表格等内容在页面中的展示效果。我们想做的不只是“能写”，而是让整理、阅读和回看自己的文字，也成为写作体验的一部分。

上一篇的“敬请期待”，现在终于有了回应。欢迎打开 Diple，试着把一组文稿编成合集，再为它换上喜欢的主题、稿纸和图标。

At the end of our [previous article](/journal/diple-speech-to-text-and-version-history/?lang=en), we wrote: “Next, we’re preparing more striking page effects and interactions for Diple… Stay tuned.” That update is finally here.

This is more than a few new settings. It changes how documents come together, how longer work is read, and how Diple looks and feels throughout the App.

## Bring separate documents into a Collection

A document might begin as a quick idea, a day in your journal, or one chapter of a draft. When those pieces grow into one subject, create a Collection, give it a name, summary, and cover, then organize the documents with chapters, numbering, and a custom reading order.

When placing an existing document in a Collection, choose “Add” or “Reference.” Add files the document inside the Collection folder. Reference leaves it in its original location while the Collection continues to show that same document, so edits stay in sync whichever way you open it.

A Collection is more than another category. Use its chapter list to jump through the work, read continuously across documents, and continue from where you stopped. When you want to take the work beyond Diple, you can also arrange the whole Collection into one exported document using its current chapters and order.

## Give all of Diple a different character

Themes reshape the atmosphere of the library, Collections, Settings, and the rest of the App. Ink Wash uses open space and layered ink tones, while Classic keeps the familiar system appearance. Moonlight, Bamboo, Dusk, Panda, Zen, and Wukong each bring their own colors, imagery, and typographic character.

Every theme is designed for both Light and Dark appearance. Settle into one quiet style for the long term, or change it with the season, your mood, or the work in front of you.

## Put Writing Paper beneath editing and reading

Themes shape the whole App; Writing Paper is made for the document page itself. Rice Paper, Graphite, Parchment, Celadon, Indigo, Blossom, and other palettes carry through body text, headings, quotes, code blocks, tables, links, and the cursor, giving editing and reading a more complete visual rhythm.

If the presets do not quite match what you want, create Writing Paper from one of your own images. It changes only the page presentation and never writes into or alters the document itself, so you can switch again whenever you like.

## Choose the icon on your Home Screen

App icons now come in different colors and characters too. From Classic, Vermilion, Celadon, and Midnight to Bamboo, Ink Brush, Pencil, and Bookmark, you can choose the Diple that feels at home on your Home Screen.

This update also adds image insertion from Photos or Files and refines how quotes, code blocks, tables, and other content appear on the page. Our goal is not only to make writing possible, but to make organizing, reading, and returning to your own words part of the writing experience.

The “Stay tuned” from our previous article now has its answer. Open Diple, bring a group of documents into a Collection, and give it a theme, Writing Paper, and icon that feel like your own.
