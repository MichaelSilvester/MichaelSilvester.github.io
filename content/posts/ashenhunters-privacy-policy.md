**最后更新及生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日**

本政策说明 Michael Silvester（下称“我们”）提供的烬夜猎人如何处理信息。本游戏的英文名称为 Ashen Hunters。隐私问题请联系 [MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com](mailto:MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com)，并在邮件中注明“烬夜猎人”。

## 1. 概述

烬夜猎人是适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 的离线单人生存动作游戏。游戏不要求注册开发者账户，不运营保存玩家进度的服务器，不集成广告、跨 App 跟踪或行为分析服务，也不出售个人信息。

游戏进度和设置在设备本地处理；购买、恢复购买和权益验证使用 Apple 服务。你主动联系支持时，我们会收到你在邮件中提供的信息。

## 2. 设备本地处理的信息

- **进度与养成：** 魂烬余额、天赋、补给库存、地图及难度解锁、武器发现记录、近期远征记录和结算记录。
- **单局存档：** 为继续远征保存的角色、地图、难度、武器与被动、宠物、补给、战斗状态及随机状态。
- **设置与选择：** 语言、音乐与音效音量、触觉反馈、伤害数字、特效、帧率、摇杆设置，以及选中的角色、外观和宠物。
- **购买访问状态：** App 通过 StoreKit 读取商品、经验证的交易、购买撤销信息和订阅有效状态，以展示商品、判断内容访问资格并恢复符合条件的购买。

上述信息用于提供游戏、保存和恢复进度及处理购买，不自动上传到开发者服务器。本地存档不代替 Apple 验证的购买凭据。

## 3. Apple 购买服务

游戏提供一次性永久内容包、终身会员，以及月度和年度自动续订会员。魂烬和单局补给是游戏内资源，当前不提供人民币购买魂烬或补给的消耗型内购。

Apple 处理付款、Apple 账户和相关交易信息。App 接收提供商品和验证权益所需的信息；我们不能获取你的支付卡号、Apple 账户密码或完整付款凭据。Apple 的处理方式见 [App Store 与隐私](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/zh-cn/app-store/)。

加载商品、购买、恢复购买和更新会员资格可能需要联网。恢复购买不会把本机游戏进度同步到另一台设备。

## 4. 系统权限与外部页面

当前游戏功能不申请相机、麦克风、照片、通讯录、定位或本地网络权限，也不请求跨 App 跟踪许可。

当你主动访问支持网站、隐私政策、使用条款或 Apple 的订阅及退款页面时，相应网站或系统服务会按其自身政策处理访问所需的信息。网站托管服务和邮件服务不属于游戏存档同步服务。

## 5. 数据保留与删除

进度、魂烬、补给、单局存档和设置保存在本机，通常保留至你删除 App 或设备上的相关数据。游戏当前不提供开发者云存档、跨设备进度同步或存档导入导出。

**卸载 App 或删除其数据可能导致本地进度丢失。“恢复购买”只能恢复符合条件的商品权益，不能找回已删除的进度、魂烬或补给。** 我们没有这些数据的云端副本，无法远程恢复。

系统备份与设备迁移由 Apple 及你的设置管理，不属于本游戏保证的恢复服务。Apple 管理其购买记录；删除 App 不会自动取消订阅。

## 6. 支持邮件与信息共享

联系支持时，我们及邮件服务提供者会处理你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，用于回复、排查问题及保留必要的支持记录。请只提供解决问题所需的信息，不要发送密码、完整付款信息或与问题无关的个人资料。

支持信息仅在处理请求及满足适用法律要求所需的期间保留。我们不为广告或营销目的出售或共享这些信息；依法需要时，可能披露实际持有的信息。我们不能直接读取仅保存在你设备上的存档。

Apple、邮件提供者及你主动访问的网站可能在你所在地区以外处理信息，其处理受各自政策约束。

## 7. 你的选择与安全

你可以调整游戏设置，在 Apple 账户中管理订阅，并通过删除 App 及其数据移除本机存档。游戏没有开发者账户，因此没有账户注销流程。对于你通过邮件提供的信息，可联系我们请求访问、更正、删除或行使适用法律赋予的其他权利。

App 使用系统存储和数据保护机制降低未授权访问风险，但不能保证绝对安全。请保护设备和 Apple 账户，排查存档问题时先保留 App 并联系支持。

## 8. 未成年人及政策更新

游戏包含幻想战斗和惊悚主题，请参考 App Store 的年龄分级。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，并在购买前取得必要的监护人同意。我们不要求玩家提交年龄或身份资料；如认为儿童通过支持邮件提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

功能或信息处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页日期；重大变化会通过适当方式告知，并在法律要求时取得同意。

**Last updated and effective: November 7, 2026**

This policy explains how Ashen Hunters, provided by Michael Silvester (“we”), handles information. The Simplified Chinese name is 烬夜猎人. For privacy questions, contact [MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com](mailto:MichaelSilvesterCN+AshenHunters@gmail.com) and mention Ashen Hunters.

## 1. Overview

Ashen Hunters is an offline, single-player survival action game for iPhone and iPad. It requires no developer account, operates no server storing player progress, integrates no advertising, cross-app tracking, or behavioral analytics services, and does not sell personal information.

Progress and settings are processed on your device. Purchases, purchase restoration, and entitlement verification use Apple services. When you contact support, we receive the information you include in your email.

## 2. Information processed on your device

- **Progress and upgrades:** Soul Ember balance, talents, supply inventory, map and difficulty unlocks, discovered weapons, recent run records, and settlement records.
- **Saved runs:** The character, map, difficulty, weapons and passives, pet, supplies, combat state, and random state needed to continue a run.
- **Settings and selections:** Language, music and sound levels, haptics, damage numbers, effects, frame rate, joystick preferences, and selected characters, skins, and pets.
- **Purchase access state:** StoreKit provides products, verified transactions, revocations, and subscription status so the app can display products, determine content access, and restore eligible purchases.

This information runs the game, saves and restores progress, and handles purchases. It is not automatically uploaded to developer servers. Local saves do not replace purchase verification by Apple.

## 3. Apple purchase services

The game offers one-time permanent packs and lifetime membership, plus monthly and annual auto-renewable memberships. Soul Embers and run supplies are in-game resources; the current game has no consumable in-app purchases selling them for money.

Apple handles payments, Apple Account information, and related transactions. The app receives information needed to offer products and verify access. We cannot obtain your payment card number, Apple Account password, or complete payment credentials. See [App Store and Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/).

Loading products, purchasing, restoring purchases, and updating membership status may require internet access. Restore Purchases does not synchronize gameplay progress with another device.

## 4. System permissions and external pages

Current game features do not request Camera, Microphone, Photos, Contacts, Location, or Local Network permission, or permission to track you across apps.

When you choose to visit support, privacy, or terms pages, or Apple's subscription and refund pages, the relevant website or system service handles access information under its own policies. Website hosting and email services do not synchronize game saves.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Progress, Soul Embers, supplies, saved runs, and settings remain on your device, normally until you delete the app or its device data. The game currently offers no developer cloud saves, cross-device progress sync, or save import and export.

**Deleting the app or its data may remove local progress. Restore Purchases restores eligible product access, not deleted progress, Soul Embers, or supplies.** We have no cloud copy and cannot recover these remotely.

System backups and device migration are managed by Apple and your settings, rather than a recovery service guaranteed by this game. Apple manages its purchase records. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

## 6. Support emails and sharing

When you contact support, we and the email provider process your email address, message, and attachments to reply, troubleshoot, and maintain necessary support records. Include only information needed to resolve the issue. Do not send passwords, complete payment details, or unrelated personal information.

Support information is retained only as needed to handle requests and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell or share it for advertising or marketing. We may disclose information we actually hold when legally required. We cannot directly access saves stored only on your device.

Apple, email providers, and websites you choose to visit may process information outside your region under their own policies.

## 7. Your choices and security

You can adjust game settings, manage subscriptions through your Apple Account, and remove local saves by deleting the app and its data. There is no developer account to delete. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other rights under applicable law for information you send by email.

The app uses system storage and data protection mechanisms to reduce unauthorized access risks, but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and Apple Account. Keep the app installed and contact support when troubleshooting saved-game problems.

## 8. Minors and policy updates

The game contains fantasy combat and horror themes. Refer to its App Store age rating. Minors should use the game with a guardian's guidance and obtain any necessary consent before purchasing. We do not require age or identity submissions. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information through support email.

We will update this page's date when features or information practices change. Material changes will be communicated appropriately, with consent obtained where required by law.
