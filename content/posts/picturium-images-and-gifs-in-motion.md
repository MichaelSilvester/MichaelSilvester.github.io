## 看图只是开始

Picturium 首先是一款顺手的 macOS 看图工具，但它不只负责把图片打开。把单张图片拖进窗口、通过按钮选择文件，或直接从 Finder 用 Picturium 打开，都可以立即开始查看；需要浏览一整批素材时，还能一次添加一个或多个文件夹。

文件夹中的图片会以缩略图列在侧栏，可以按名称、修改时间、创建时间、大小和类型排序，也可以只看 GIF。挑素材、找动图、在相邻图片之间切换，都不必反复打开 Finder 和多个窗口。静态图片与 GIF 放在同一个浏览流程里，看到动图时还能直接播放或暂停。

真正有意思的地方，是找到一张图片之后，可以顺手继续处理，而不用为了一个简单想法切换到更复杂的软件。

## 裁剪 GIF，画面变小，动画还在

很多工具可以裁剪图片，但处理 GIF 时往往只留下其中一帧，原本会动的内容就变成了静态图。Picturium 的裁剪会保留完整动画：框选真正需要的区域后，输出画面虽然变小，帧数与每一帧的播放节奏仍然保持不变。

这在制作聊天表情、截取录屏中的重点区域、去掉动图四周多余留白时尤其方便。你不需要先把 GIF 拆成许多帧，逐张裁剪后再重新合成；操作方式和裁剪普通图片一样，结果却仍是一张正常播放的 GIF。

## 图片叠图片，动图也能叠动图

“叠图”让一张图片不再只是最终结果，也可以成为下一次组合的底图。可以从缩略图侧栏把素材拖到画布，从 Finder 直接拖入，或一次选择多张图片。每一张加入的素材都会成为独立图层，可以单独移动、旋转和缩放。

静态图片叠加可以用来快速拼贴、加贴纸或放入角标；把 GIF 叠在静态底图上，则能让原本不动的画面出现一个动态焦点。更特别的是，Picturium 支持动图叠动图：底图和每一个 GIF 图层都按自己的节奏播放，不会把其中一个动画冻结成单帧。

这意味着两个速度不同、帧数不同的 GIF 也能被放进同一张结果里。它们在画布上各自动起来，组合会随着播放不断出现新的瞬间。原本只是几张零散素材，经过位置、大小和角度的调整，就可能变成一张新的表情、动态拼贴或小动画。

只要组合中包含动画，保存或导出时需要选择 GIF 格式，才能保留动效。

## 标注、文字和马赛克也在手边

除了裁剪与叠图，编辑模式还提供矩形、椭圆、直线、箭头和自由画笔，适合给截图圈出重点、补充说明或随手涂鸦。文字可以调整字体与字号；需要隐藏人脸、账号、证件号码等信息时，可以直接用马赛克涂抹。

添加到画布上的元素并不是画下去就无法修改。它们可以分别选中，再移动、旋转或缩放，并且支持撤销与重做。无论是认真整理一张说明图，还是随手给朋友做一个有趣的图片回复，都可以边试边改。

## 从浏览到创作，不打断思路

Picturium 把这些功能放在一起的意义，并不是把看图工具变成一套庞大的专业软件，而是缩短“看见素材”和“做出结果”之间的距离：浏览文件夹时发现合适的图片，可以立即裁剪；看到一个 GIF 中有趣的局部，可以保留动效截出来；想到两个动图放在一起会很好玩，也可以直接拖到画布上试一试。

完成后，可以选择“保存”覆盖原文件，也可以“导出”为新文件、保留原图。保存通常无法撤销，因此处理重要或唯一的素材时，先导出一份副本会更安心。

## Viewing is only the beginning

Picturium starts as a convenient image viewer for macOS, but it does more than open a picture. Drag a file into the window, choose one with the Open button, or open it directly from Finder. When you need to browse a larger collection, add one or more folders at once.

Images from those folders appear as thumbnails in the sidebar. Sort them by name, modification date, creation date, size, or kind, or filter the list down to GIFs. Finding an animation, choosing source material, and moving between nearby images no longer requires repeated trips through Finder and a pile of separate windows. Still images and GIFs share the same browsing flow, with direct play and pause controls whenever an animation appears.

The more interesting part begins after you find an image: you can continue working with it immediately instead of moving a simple idea into a much heavier editor.

## Crop a GIF and keep it moving

Plenty of tools can crop an image, but some reduce a GIF to a single frame in the process. Picturium preserves the complete animation. Draw the area you want to keep and the picture becomes smaller while its frame count and per-frame timing stay intact.

That makes it useful for creating chat reactions, focusing attention on one part of a screen recording, or removing empty space around an animation. There is no need to split the GIF into frames, crop every image, and assemble it again. The action feels like cropping an ordinary picture, but the result is still a normally playing GIF.

## Stack images—and stack animations too

Stacking turns an image from a finished result into a canvas for a new combination. Drag media from the thumbnail sidebar or Finder, or select several images at once. Every addition becomes its own layer, with independent position, rotation, and scale.

Still-image stacking is handy for quick collages, stickers, and corner badges. Put a GIF over a still background and an otherwise static scene gains a moving point of focus. More unusually, Picturium supports GIF-on-GIF stacking: the base and every animated layer continue playing at their own pace instead of freezing one animation into a single frame.

Even GIFs with different speeds and frame counts can therefore share one result. As they move independently on the canvas, their combination keeps producing new moments. A few unrelated source files can become a new reaction image, animated collage, or tiny piece of motion simply by changing their placement, size, and angle.

Whenever a composition contains animation, save or export it as a GIF to keep the motion.

## Annotation, text, and mosaic tools stay close by

Editing mode also provides rectangles, ellipses, lines, arrows, and a freehand pen for highlighting a screenshot, adding an explanation, or simply drawing over an image. Text supports font and size controls. When a face, account name, ID number, or other private detail needs to be hidden, paint over it with the mosaic tool.

Elements are not permanently fixed the moment they are added. Select each one independently to move, rotate, or resize it, with undo and redo available throughout. The same tools work for a carefully prepared instructional image or a quick, playful reply to a friend.

## Move from browsing to making without losing the idea

Putting these features together is not about turning an image viewer into a huge professional suite. It is about shortening the distance between seeing useful source material and making something from it. Find the right picture while browsing a folder and crop it immediately. Notice a funny moment inside a GIF and keep that moving fragment. Wonder what two animations would look like together and drag them onto the same canvas to find out.

When the result is ready, choose Save to replace the original or Export to create a new file and leave the source untouched. Saving over a file usually cannot be undone, so exporting a copy first is the safer choice for anything important or irreplaceable.
