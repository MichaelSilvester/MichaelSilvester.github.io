## 1. 概述

PrimePlayer 的设计与数据处理方式如下：

- 不要求注册账户或登录；
- 不使用开发者运营的云服务器；
- 不包含广告、用户跟踪或行为分析；
- 不使用广告标识符；
- 不会把你的视频、照片、播放历史或文件夹信息上传到开发者运营的服务器；
- 不会向数据经纪商出售、出租或提供你的个人信息。

你的媒体、播放历史、播放列表和设置主要存储在设备本地。当你使用网络播放、下载、本地网络传输或 Apple App 内购买时，相关信息可能会传输到你选择的网络地址、同一本地网络中的另一台设备或 Apple 服务。

## 2. App 处理的信息

### 2.1 媒体与媒体库信息

为提供视频导入、播放、整理、下载和导出功能，本 App 可能在你的设备本地处理以下信息：

- 你导入或下载的视频、字幕和封面图片；
- 文件名、文件类型、文件大小、视频时长及相关媒体元数据；
- 文件夹名称、播放列表、收藏和排序信息；
- 播放进度、播放时间和播放历史；
- 你添加的远程媒体 URL。

这些信息存储在 App 沙盒或本地数据库中，不会上传到开发者运营的服务器。

### 2.2 设置与偏好

本 App 会在你的设备本地保存语言、外观、播放速度、字幕样式、网络下载偏好、画中画偏好、播放历史设置以及其他功能偏好。

### 2.3 文件夹与播放列表保护

启用密码保护时：

- 密码存储在 iOS 钥匙串中；
- 密码提示存储在 App 本地数据库中；
- 我们无法远程查看或恢复你的密码。

请勿在密码提示中包含身份证件号码、联系方式或其他敏感个人信息。

使用 Face ID 或 Touch ID 时，身份验证由 iOS 执行。本 App 只会收到成功、失败或取消等验证结果，不会访问、存储或上传你的面部或指纹数据。

### 2.4 会员与购买信息

PrimePlayer Pro 的订阅、购买、计费和恢复购买由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。

本 App 只读取用于判断是否应解锁 Pro 功能所必需的产品标识符、购买结果和会员权益状态。

我们无法访问你的支付卡号、支付密码或完整支付凭据。Apple 会依据 Apple 隐私政策处理这些信息。

## 3. 系统权限

### 3.1 系统相册访问

本 App 可能请求读取系统相册或向其中添加项目的权限，以便：

- 显示并导入系统相册中的视频；
- 选择图片作为视频封面；
- 将截图、动态 GIF 或视频保存到系统相册。

本 App 可以访问的内容取决于你在 iOS 中授予完全访问、有限访问还是不允许访问。如果某个项目存储在 iCloud 中，iOS 可能会通过 Apple 服务下载该项目。

### 3.2 本地网络访问

本地网络权限用于：

- 播放本地网络中可用的媒体流；
- 通过网页浏览器，在 PrimePlayer 和同一 Wi-Fi 网络中的另一台设备之间传输文件。

只有在你主动启用相关功能时，本地网络传输才会运行。文件会在你的设备与访问本地传输页面的设备之间直接传输，不会经过开发者运营的服务器。

### 3.3 生物识别验证

Face ID 或 Touch ID 仅用于验证你是否有权访问或管理受保护的文件夹或播放列表。

生物识别信息由 iOS 安全隔区和系统身份验证服务管理。本 App 无法访问底层面部或指纹信息。

你可以随时在 iOS“设置”中更改这些权限。拒绝或撤回某项权限只会影响需要该权限的功能。

## 4. 网络播放、下载与本地传输

当你输入远程媒体 URL 或播放网络媒体时，本 App 会按照你的指示向相应服务器发送请求。该服务器可能接收正常网络通信所需的信息，包括：

- 你的 IP 地址；
- 请求时间；
- 请求路径；
- 协议与连接信息。

这些服务器由你或第三方运营。我们无法控制其隐私或数据处理方式。连接前请查看相关服务的隐私政策，并确认来源值得信任。

部分受支持的媒体协议（包括 HTTP 和 FTP）可能不提供加密传输。通过未加密协议传输的信息可能被网络中的其他方截获。请尽可能使用 HTTPS、FTPS 等加密协议。

基于浏览器的本地传输使用本地 HTTP 服务。请仅在可信的 Wi-Fi 网络中使用此功能，尽可能保持身份验证开启，不要将页面显示的用户名或密码告诉未经授权的人，并在传输完成后关闭传输页面。

## 5. 信息共享与披露

我们不会为广告、营销或数据经纪目的共享你的信息。

信息仅可能在以下情况下传输或披露：

- **Apple 服务：** Apple 处理购买、订阅、恢复购买和 App Store 服务；
- **你选择的网络服务：** 当你播放或下载远程媒体时，请求会发送到你指定的服务器；
- **本地网络接收设备：** 当你发起基于浏览器的导入或导出时，文件会传入或传出相关设备；
- **法律要求：** 我们可能遵守适用法律、法律程序、法院命令或有权公共机关提出的合法要求。

本 App 使用 VLCKit 进行媒体解码并提供部分网络播放能力。当前集成未配置广告或分析服务。

## 6. 存储与保留

本 App 不提供由开发者运营的云账户或云同步服务。相关数据主要存储在：

- App 沙盒；
- 本地 SwiftData 数据库；
- iOS 钥匙串；
- 由 App 与其共享扩展共同使用的本地 App Group 容器。

媒体、播放历史和设置通常会保留到你删除相关内容、清除历史或卸载本 App 为止。App 或操作系统可能在适当情况下删除缓存和临时文件。

钥匙串项目和 Apple 购买记录依照 iOS 与 Apple 政策管理。若要尽可能删除本地数据，请先删除媒体、清除播放历史并移除密码保护，再卸载本 App。

## 7. 你的权利与选择

你可以通过以下方式管理本 App 处理的信息：

- 在 App 中查看、编辑或删除媒体、文件夹、播放列表和播放历史；
- 导出存储在 App 中的媒体文件；
- 关闭播放历史或其他可选功能；
- 移除文件夹或播放列表保护；
- 在 iOS“设置”中撤回系统相册、本地网络或生物识别权限；
- 在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消 PrimePlayer Pro 订阅；
- 联系我们，请求访问、更正、删除、限制处理或其他隐私相关协助。

本 App 不提供账户系统，因此没有账户删除流程。由于大多数信息存储在你的设备本地，我们通常无法远程访问或删除这些信息。

## 8. 安全

我们采用与本 App 功能相适应的措施，包括：

- 通过 iOS 应用沙盒隔离本地文件；
- 在 iOS 钥匙串中存储保护密码；
- 使用 iOS 系统服务进行生物识别验证；
- 为本地网络传输提供可选身份验证；
- 将系统权限限制在与 App 功能相关的用途内。

任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。请保护好设备密码和本地传输凭据，并避免通过不可信网络传输敏感内容。

## 1. Overview

PrimePlayer is designed and handles data as follows:

- Does not require account registration or sign-in;
- Does not use developer-operated cloud servers;
- Does not include advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics;
- Does not use advertising identifiers;
- Does not upload your videos, photos, playback history, or folder information to developer-operated servers;
- Does not sell, rent, or provide your personal information to data brokers.

Your media, playback history, playlists, and settings are primarily stored locally on your device. When you use network playback, downloading, local-network transfer, or Apple in-app purchases, relevant information may be transmitted to a network address you select, another device on the same local network, or an Apple service.

## 2. Information Processed by the App

### 2.1 Media and Media Library Information

To provide video importing, playback, organization, downloading, and exporting features, the App may process the following information locally on your device:

- Videos, subtitles, and cover images you import or download;
- File names, file types, file sizes, video durations, and related media metadata;
- Folder names, playlists, favorites, and sorting information;
- Playback progress, playback time, and playback history;
- Remote media URLs that you add.

This information is stored in the App’s sandbox or local database and is not uploaded to developer-operated servers.

### 2.2 Settings and Preferences

The App stores settings such as language, appearance, playback speed, subtitle style, network download preferences, Picture in Picture preferences, playback history settings, and other feature preferences locally on your device.

### 2.3 Folder and Playlist Protection

When you enable password protection:

- Passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain;
- Password hints are stored in the App’s local database;
- We cannot remotely view or recover your passwords.

Do not include identity numbers, contact details, or other sensitive personal information in password hints.

When you use Face ID or Touch ID, authentication is performed by iOS. The App receives only the authentication result, such as success, failure, or cancellation. The App does not access, store, or upload your facial or fingerprint data.

### 2.4 Membership and Purchase Information

PrimePlayer Pro subscriptions, purchases, billing, and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through StoreKit.

The App reads only the product identifier, purchase result, and membership entitlement status required to determine whether Pro features should be unlocked.

We do not have access to your payment card number, payment password, or complete payment credentials. Apple processes this information under the Apple Privacy Policy.

## 3. System Permissions

### 3.1 Photo Library Access

The App may request permission to read from or add items to your Photo Library in order to:

- Display and import videos from your Photo Library;
- Select an image to use as a video cover;
- Save screenshots, animated GIFs, or videos to your Photo Library.

The content available to the App depends on whether you grant full access, limited access, or no access in iOS. If an item is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it through Apple’s services.

### 3.2 Local Network Access

Local Network permission is used to:

- Play media streams available on your local network;
- Transfer files between PrimePlayer and another device on the same Wi-Fi network through a web browser.

Local-network transfer operates only when you actively enable the relevant feature. Files are transferred directly between your device and the device accessing the local transfer page. They do not pass through developer-operated servers.

### 3.3 Biometric Authentication

Face ID or Touch ID is used only to verify whether you are authorized to access or manage protected folders or playlists.

Biometric information is managed by the iOS Secure Enclave and system authentication services. The App cannot access the underlying facial or fingerprint information.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Refusing or withdrawing a permission affects only the features that require that permission.

## 4. Network Playback, Downloads, and Local Transfers

When you enter a remote media URL or play network media, the App sends requests to the corresponding server at your direction. That server may receive information required for normal network communication, including:

- Your IP address;
- The request time;
- The requested path;
- Protocol and connection information.

These servers are operated by you or third parties. We do not control their privacy or data-handling practices. Review the relevant service’s privacy policy and confirm that the source is trustworthy before connecting.

Some supported media protocols, including HTTP and FTP, may not provide encrypted transmission. Information transferred through an unencrypted protocol may be intercepted by other parties on the network. Use encrypted protocols such as HTTPS or FTPS whenever possible.

Browser-based local transfer uses a local HTTP service. Use this feature only on a trusted Wi-Fi network, keep authentication enabled when possible, do not share the displayed username or password with unauthorized people, and close the transfer page when the transfer is complete.

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your information for advertising, marketing, or data-brokerage purposes.

Information may be transmitted or disclosed only in the following circumstances:

- **Apple services:** Apple processes purchases, subscriptions, purchase restoration, and App Store services;
- **Network services selected by you:** Requests are sent to the server you specify when you play or download remote media;
- **Local-network recipient devices:** Files are transferred to or from the relevant device when you initiate a browser-based import or export;
- **Legal requirements:** We may comply with applicable laws, legal proceedings, court orders, or lawful requests from authorized public authorities.

The App uses VLCKit for media decoding and certain network playback capabilities. The current integration does not configure advertising or analytics services.

## 6. Storage and Retention

The App does not provide a developer-operated cloud account or cloud synchronization service. Relevant data is primarily stored in:

- The App’s sandbox;
- A local SwiftData database;
- The iOS Keychain;
- A local App Group container shared between the App and its Share Extension.

Media, playback history, and settings are generally retained until you delete the relevant content, clear the history, or uninstall the App. Cache and temporary files may be removed by the App or the operating system when appropriate.

Keychain items and Apple purchase records are managed according to iOS and Apple policies. To remove as much local data as possible, delete your media, clear playback history, and remove password protection before uninstalling the App.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You can manage information processed by the App by:

- Viewing, editing, or deleting media, folders, playlists, and playback history within the App;
- Exporting media files stored in the App;
- Disabling playback history or other optional features;
- Removing folder or playlist protection;
- Withdrawing Photo Library, Local Network, or biometric permissions in iOS Settings;
- Managing or canceling a PrimePlayer Pro subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings;
- Contacting us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other privacy-related assistance.

The App does not provide an account system, so there is no account deletion process. Because most information is stored locally on your device, we generally cannot remotely access or delete that information.

## 8. Security

We use measures appropriate to the App’s functionality, including:

- Isolating local files through the iOS application sandbox;
- Storing protection passwords in the iOS Keychain;
- Using iOS system services for biometric authentication;
- Providing optional authentication for local-network transfers;
- Limiting system permissions to purposes related to App functionality.

No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device passcode and local-transfer credentials, and avoid transferring sensitive content over untrusted networks.
