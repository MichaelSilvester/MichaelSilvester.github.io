在[合集、主题与稿纸](/journal/diple-collections-themes-and-writing-paper/)之后，Diple 又加入了一批围绕日常写作的功能。文稿之间可以互相连接，表格和图片可以直接在页面里处理，写好的内容也有了更多分享与保存方式。

## 让文稿彼此连接

写旅行计划时，可以链接到行李清单；整理读书笔记时，也可以把人物、章节和自己的想法连在一起。在正文中输入 [[文稿名]]，或从格式工具栏选择“链接到文稿”，就能建立文稿之间的跳转。

输入名称时可以从候选文稿中选择，也可以链接到某个标题位置，为链接设置不同的显示文字。打开一篇文稿后，还能通过“反向链接”查看哪些文稿引用了它，并回到具体的引用位置。原本散落在不同文件夹里的内容，由此多了一条按思路查找的路径。

## 表格、公式与图片，都能放进笔记

记账、做计划或整理对照资料时，可以直接编辑 Markdown 表格。

为了兼顾文稿在其他 Markdown App 中的通用展示效果，“插入表格”和“调整表格”的列数设置最多提供 6 列。需要更宽或更长的表格时，可以在单元格中输入 ?A，再按箭头方向继续增加列或行；表格超过页面宽度后，可以左右滑动查看。

在单元格中输入 ?S，可以选择方向或指定范围求和；?D 用于删除行列。求和结果会写回当前单元格，便于继续整理。

学习笔记里的公式也可以用 TeX 语法书写，支持行内公式和独立成行的公式，在阅读排版中显示。

从相册或“文件”插入本地图片时，可以先裁剪、添加文字或填写说明。回到文稿后，还能调整图片的展示大小；需要细改时，再打开图片编辑界面。在预览模式下，点击图片可以全屏查看，也可以将预览图片保存到相册。

## 选一款适合内容的字体

在“设置—编辑器—文稿字体”中，可以选择系统字体、圆体、衬线体、等宽体，以及设备上可用的其他字体。字体以自身样式展示，可以先看看正文的感觉，再决定使用哪一款。

所选字体用于编辑与阅读，也可以延续到 PDF、Word、HTML 和图片导出。代码仍使用等宽字体；PDF 会根据字体授权决定是否嵌入，Word 则由打开文件的设备匹配可用字体。稿纸、主题与字体可以搭配使用，让日记、学习笔记和长篇草稿各有适合的呈现。

## 分享一张图片，或带走完整文稿

除了 Markdown、TXT、Word 和 PDF，现在也可以导出 HTML、图片和 Diple 文稿。

- 想把排好版的一段内容发给别人，可以按阅读样式导出图片，或直接保存到相册。
- 想在浏览器中查看内容，可以选择 HTML。
- 想在另一台设备的 Diple 中继续编辑，可以导出 Diple 文稿，将正文与引用的本地图片、视频一起打包，再在另一台设备上打开导入。

批量导出、整文件夹导出和整册合集导出也可以继续使用，不必逐篇处理。

## 给文稿留一份备份，也能直接迁移到新设备

在“工具—数据备份”中，可以把文稿、文件夹、合集与历史版本打包保存，为备份设置密码和提示，再导出到自己选择的位置。恢复时按规则合并内容，遇到不同修改可以保留副本，避免直接替换整座文稿库。

两台设备都在身边时，可以打开“工具—数据迁移”，连接附近的 Diple，选择全部或部分内容直接传输。文稿中的本地图片、备注和历史版本，以及合集的章节、封面和引用关系，都能随内容一起迁移。数据在设备之间加密传输，无需连接同一个 Wi-Fi，也不占用 iCloud 存储空间；完成前请保持两台设备上的 Diple 都在前台。

## 从系统搜索与 Siri 回到写作

开启“在 Spotlight 中显示”后，可以在系统搜索中用文稿标题、正文关键词或合集名称查找内容。Siri 和快捷指令则可以新建文稿、打开指定文稿、追加文字或打开合集。

系统搜索和 Siri 文稿访问可以在“设置—数据安全”中分别管理，受保护的内容不会向 Spotlight 或 Siri 开放。Diple 也支持 32 种界面语言，可以选择自己熟悉的语言继续写作。

从连接两篇笔记，到把整个文稿库带到新设备，这些更新都围绕同一件事：让已经写下来的内容，更方便整理、回看和继续使用。

Since [Collections, themes, and Writing Paper](/journal/diple-collections-themes-and-writing-paper/?lang=en), Diple has gained more tools for everyday writing. Documents can link to one another, tables and images can be edited on the page, and finished work has more ways to be shared and saved.

## Connect your documents

Link a travel plan to a packing list, or connect people, chapters, and your own thoughts across reading notes. Type [[Document Name]] in the body, or choose “Link to Document” from the formatting toolbar, to create a link to another document.

Choose from matching documents as you type, link to a particular heading, or give a link different display text. Open Backlinks to see which documents refer to the current one and return to the exact reference. Notes spread across different folders become easier to follow by the ideas that connect them.

## Bring tables, formulas, and images into your notes

Edit Markdown tables directly when keeping accounts, making plans, or comparing information.

To help tables display well across other Markdown apps, the Insert Table and Adjust Table controls offer up to six columns. For a wider or longer table, enter ?A in a cell and use the arrows to keep adding columns or rows. Tables wider than the page can be scrolled horizontally.

Enter ?S in a cell to sum values in a direction or a selected range, or ?D to delete a row or column. The sum is written into the current cell so you can keep working.

Study notes can also include formulas written in TeX syntax, with both inline and display formulas shown in the reading layout.

When inserting a local image from Photos or Files, crop it, add text, or write a description first. Back in the document, adjust its display size or reopen the image editor for more changes. In Preview mode, tap an image to view it full screen, with an option to save it to Photos.

## Choose a font that suits your writing

In Settings > Editor > Document Font, choose a system, rounded, serif, or monospaced font, or another font available on your device. Each font appears in its own style so you can preview the feel of the text before choosing.

Your choice applies to editing and reading, and can carry through to PDF, Word, HTML, and image exports. Code stays monospaced. PDF embedding depends on the font’s permissions, while Word uses an available matching font on the device opening the file. Combine fonts with Writing Paper and themes to suit a journal, study notes, or a longer draft.

## Share an image or take the whole document with you

Alongside Markdown, TXT, Word, and PDF, you can now export HTML, images, and Diple Documents.

- Export an image in the reading layout to share a formatted passage, or save it directly to Photos.
- Choose HTML to view the content in a browser.
- Export a Diple Document to keep editing in Diple on another device. It packages the text with its referenced local images and videos, ready to open and import there.

Batch export, whole-folder export, and whole-Collection export remain available, so you can work with several pieces at once.

## Keep a backup or transfer to another device

In Tools > Data Backup, package documents, folders, Collections, and Version History, add a password and hint, and export the backup to a location you choose. Restoring merges content according to the restore rules, with copies retained for conflicting edits rather than replacing the entire library.

When both devices are nearby, open Tools > Data Migration, connect to the other Diple, and transfer all or selected content directly. Local images, notes, Version History, and Collection chapters, covers, and references travel with the documents. Transfers are encrypted between devices, require no shared Wi-Fi network, and use no iCloud storage. Keep Diple in the foreground on both devices until the transfer finishes.

## Return to writing through system search and Siri

Turn on “Show in Spotlight” to find content by document title, words in the body, or Collection name from system search. Siri and Shortcuts can create a document, open a named document, append text, or open a Collection.

Manage system search and Siri document access separately in Settings > Data Security. Protected content stays unavailable to Spotlight and Siri. Diple also supports 32 interface languages, so you can keep writing in a language you know.

From connecting two notes to moving an entire library to another device, these updates make the words you have already written easier to organize, revisit, and keep using.
